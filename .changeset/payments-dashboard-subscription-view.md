The payments console can open one subscription in full (`#/subscriptions/<id>`): its state, plan and amount, trial and period dates, cancellation and renewal failures, the customer it bills, the payments charged for it, the deliveries naming them, and what people did to it. Rows of the subscriptions list open it, the customers screen links to a customer's subscriptions (`#/subscriptions?customer=<id>`), and a subscription found by the Ctrl/Cmd-K search opens it directly.
